📌 5 steps scan · join · wait

1
Scan the QR code with your phone camera.
2
Enter name, phone, and reason, then tap "Join queue".
3
Watch your live position — "X ahead" updates in real time.
4
When it's your turn the screen says "It's your turn!".
5
Go to the service desk with your ticket number.
